1. The accept-time snapshot

When a technician accepts (or a manager assigns), everything that depends on that technician is frozen into tickets.accept_snapshot in the same guarded UPDATE: their franchisee, their type, the travel allowance from their base, and the speed terms. A base moved or a rate raised later never restates the accepted job; releasing or reassigning clears it and the next accept stamps it afresh. Old tickets read as NULL, which means "nothing to pay".



FieldMeaning


travel.reasonnull, no_base_location or no_customer_point. A missing point is 0 km with a reason - the job is still accepted (TA2, TA8).


travel.basePoint.sourcepincode | gps | map | manual. The customer point's source is ticket (a picked map result) or pincode (its centre).

2. Travel allowance calculator




Distance is one way, base to customer, straight line × road factor (TA1). Partial steps are dropped. amount = min(max, floor(max(0, km − free) / step) × rate) - computed in integer tenths of a kilometre, so the km shown is the km paid for (W1).

3. Speed outcome calculator




Clock: starts at the later of the latest assigned event and the confirmed slot start → the completed event, minus time in Awaiting Part; a reschedule does not reset it (S3). A job accepted days before its slot is therefore not penalised for the wait: the clock only starts when the slot does. If the slot starts after completion the clock reads 0 h (clamped). Duration ≤ reward within pays a reward; > penalty after charges a penalty; in between, nothing (S4). Comparison uses exact seconds, never the rounded figure (W4).





























Times are IST wall-clock here; the API stores UTC instants. Overlapping waits are merged and clipped to the window.

4. What the customer's confirmation writes




One transaction on customer closure: the ticket closes, then the payout (freelancers only), the travel row, then the speed settlement - all or nothing. The rows below follow the two calculators above.












    

  • Every row carries franchisee_id = the technician's franchisee at write time. travel is not a pool kind; speed_reward and speed_penalty are, and a speed penalty counts against the monthly cancellation cap and is reversible like a penalty.
    • 

  • Zero amounts write no row (the ledger CHECK is amount > 0); a speed settlement still records its measurement as a speed_settled event, with no rupee amounts, hidden from vendors.
    • 

  • Force-close writes no speed money (S5, planner default); travel is the manager's number, pre-filled with the frozen amount and capped at the frozen maximum; 0 writes no row.
