1. Company, home franchisee, external franchisees

Every company has exactly one home franchisee (internal, created with the company, never deleted, never made external - F1). It stores no pincode rows: it owns every active pincode nobody else owns (F4), so a ticket can never be without a franchisee. Other franchisees own ranges that never overlap (F3).

404 or 403?

404 - the caller must not learn it exists. Another company's row, for anyone. Another franchisee's technician, ledger row, redemption, request or ticket, for a franchise principal (the franchise wall is a second wall inside the company). A guessed ticket id outside a franchisee's ownership. Request attachments, always.
403 - they can already see it, they just cannot change it. A staff write on a visible non-home technician (including PUT /technicians/{id}/base-location): "Managed by SkyFix Services - only its franchisee admin can change this technician". A missing feature key or rank floor. Also a read of a money screen that is not theirs: a 403, not a fake empty answer.
422 or 400 - not a wall: PINCODE_OUTSIDE_FRANCHISEE is always 422. Staff are told who owns the codes ("Outside Acme Home: 500032, 500033 (SkyFix Services). Pick pincodes Acme Home owns."); a franchise caller is not: "Outside your franchisee: 500032, 500033. Pick pincodes it owns."
