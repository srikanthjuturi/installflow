Mocks Task 13 §5.4 (Allowance rules — speed matrix; contract §4.2; copy §5.10 “Speed”). Pick a type and a category or product, set the reward / penalty hours and rupees. Rules are real in this mock: add, edit, remove and the “try a duration” tester all work.
Finish fast, earn a reward; finish late, pay a penalty. The clock runs from accepting the job to pressing Complete, minus time waiting for a part. It starts at the later of accepting and the start of the customer’s confirmed time, so a job taken days ahead is not penalised for waiting. A product’s rule beats its category’s, and the deepest category wins.
